ICLR 2024 – acceptance statistics

Total submissions:
- 7,304 submissions in total.
- Overall reviewer scores across all submissions:
  min 1.00, max 9.00, mean 5.11, std 1.26.

  min 3.60, max 9.00, mean 6.44, std 0.70.

Withdrawn papers:
- 1,655 withdrawn papers (22.66% of all submissions).
- Reviewer score statistics (withdraw tier):
  min 1.00, max 7.50, mean 4.02, std 0.80.

Desk rejections:
- 54 desk rejects (0.74% of all submissions).

How to interpret these statistics:
- Total = Accept + Reject + Withdraw + Desk Reject − Post-decision Withdraw (if any).
- The rate for each status (accept, reject, withdraw, desk reject) is: count for that status / total submissions.
- For each decision tier, reviewer scores are averaged per paper first, then we report the distribution over these per-paper averages:
  * min: lowest average reviewer score among papers in the tier.
  * max: highest average reviewer score in the tier.
  * mean: average of all per-paper mean scores in the tier.
  * std: standard deviation of those per-paper mean scores.
